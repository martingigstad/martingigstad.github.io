Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-11.

Who we are

Hillset is made and sold by Martin Gigstad, John Strandruds vei 1, 1366 Lysaker, organisation number 938 201 935. We are responsible for the personal data described here (the "controller" under the GDPR, the EU's data protection law).

Contact: martincgigstad@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account.
  • We don't track you and show no ads.
  • Your programs stay on your phone.
  • A little data leaves your phone when the app starts (to check for updates and whether you have Pro) and when you buy or restore Pro.

What stays on your phone

Your programs and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. The app also remembers on your phone whether you have Pro.

This storage is strictly necessary for the app to work, so it needs no consent (ekomloven § 3-15, the Norwegian Electronic Communications Act).

When you delete the app, all of it is deleted from your phone. Your phone's own backups, such as iCloud, may still hold a copy.

What leaves your phone

Checking and buying Pro. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore Pro, the app asks RevenueCat, the service that handles purchases for us, whether you have Pro. It sends:

  • the purchase receipt from Apple, if you have bought Pro
  • a random ID the app creates, not linked to your name
  • your app version, iOS version, language and App Store country
  • your IP address, as with any connection over the internet

Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or your Apple Account details.

RevenueCat is our processor: it uses this data only on our instructions. We need it to give you Pro if you have it, and to let you buy it (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). RevenueCat's servers are in the USA, and the transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards.

The update check at launch. Each time the app starts, it asks Expo, the service that delivers app updates, whether an update is available. Expo receives:

  • the app version and the update version
  • the platform, such as iOS
  • a random installation ID, which Expo uses to deliver updates and count installations
  • your IP address
  • the technical details every web request carries, such as your phone's language and iOS version

Expo handles this data to deliver updates to the app. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in fixing problems quickly (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Expo's servers are in the USA, and Expo is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can read about the framework at dataprivacyframework.gov.

Email you send us. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail), which may store it in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

No law requires you to give us data. To buy or restore Pro, the app must send the data above to RevenueCat. If the app can't connect, it still works, but you can't buy Pro or get updates. We make no automated decisions about you.

What we never do

  • No analytics or tracking tools in the app
  • No ads
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location or health data
  • We never sell data

If you allowed it in your iPhone's settings, Apple shares crash reports and summary usage figures with us.

How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete it or the app.
  • RevenueCat: as long as needed for you to restore Pro, or until we ask RevenueCat to delete it.
  • Expo: only as long as Expo needs it to run the service, under Expo's own privacy policy.
  • Email: as long as we need it to help you, then we delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data we have about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, get a copy to take elsewhere, and object to how we use it. Write to martincgigstad@gmail.com.

We don't know who you are, so we may need your help to find your data, for example a purchase receipt.

You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no). We'd be glad to hear from you first.

Children

Hillset is made for a general audience and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version comes with an app update and the date at the top changes.
