Questions

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple account, open Settings and tap Restore purchase.

Why do the cues stop when the screen locks?

Spoken cues with the screen locked are part of Pro. On the free version, keep the screen on while you run.

Where are my programs stored?

Only on your phone. If you delete the app, its programs are deleted with it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
